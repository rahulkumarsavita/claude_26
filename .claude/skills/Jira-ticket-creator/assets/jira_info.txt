## Description
As a customer, I want to browse the product catalog and add multiple items to my shopping cart so that I can review prices, adjust quantities, and verify totals prior to checkout.

## Acceptance Criteria

### Adding Items
- Hovering over a product card in /products displays an "Add to cart" overlay button.
- Clicking "Add to cart" triggers a modal: "Your product has been added to cart." with options to Continue Shopping or View Cart.

### Cart State Verification
- Navigating to /view_cart displays item thumbnail, Name, Category, Price, Quantity, and Total Price.
- Calculated Total: Item Total must equal Unit Price x Quantity for each row.

### Cart Persistence
- Adding Product ID 1 ($500) and Product ID 2 ($400) results in an overall cart subtotal of $900.

### Item Removal
- Clicking the X (Delete) button removes the item dynamically without requiring a full page refresh.
